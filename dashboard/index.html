Definitions, data quality and assumptions
  • Net revenue is the sum of every order line, including the "Fee Discounts & Client Returns" category. Gross revenue leaves that category out. The two differ by about 1%.
  • Orders count distinct order IDs. An order has 1 to 9 category lines, one date and one ZIP. AOV is net revenue divided by orders.
  • Revenue per ZIP divides net revenue by the ZIP territories an agent or region covers. Every ZIP has 299 or 300 orders in the data, so it is the fair way to compare agents with 1 to 6 ZIPs.
  • Jul–Nov vs Jan–Jun compares average monthly revenue, because the two periods have 5 and 6 full months. December (1–6 Dec, 4 trading days) is partial and is left out of every comparison.
  • ZIP 00965 (Puerto Rico) has no region or agent in the reference table. Its 647 lines ($76,608) are kept and shown as "Unassigned (PR)".
  • Cleaning fixed team name case ("team 1" to "Team 1"), trailing spaces in agent regions, the agent ID type and lost leading zeros in ZIP codes. No rows were removed and every total reconciles to the source to the cent.
  • 2,367 lines (17.5%) have revenue 0 and 121 lines outside the discount category are negative (refunds, $−12,936.73 in total). Both are kept as recorded.
  • Patterns here describe the data; they do not show what causes them. There are no targets or quotas in the data, so the page compares against company averages and earlier months.
